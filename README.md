# ASCLEA-LMS: AI-Driven Feedback & CV Cheating Mitigation

**ASCLEA-LMS** is a **Learning Management System (LMS)** built for a review center client. It helps manage programs, courses, quizzes, admissions, payments, students, administration, archives, and backups efficiently.  

## Key Features

### 1. Quiz Management with Cheating Mitigation
- Students take quizzes with their **camera open**.  
- The system monitors for **suspicious activity** in real-time:  
  - Detects **objects** in the camera feed.  
  - Monitors if the student is **looking away** or **missing from view**.  
  - Takes **screenshots of suspicious activity** for instructor review.  
- Includes **tab detection** to prevent switching away from the quiz.  

### 2. AI-Driven Feedback
- Provides **personalized AI feedback** tuned for the review center’s style.  
- Generates **individual question feedback** to help students improve.  
- Students receive **actionable insights** after completing the quiz.  

### 3. Administrative & Student Management
- **Program and Course Management**: Manage all courses and programs offered.  
- **Admission Management**: Handle student enrollment and profiles.  
- **Payments & History Management**: Track payments and financial history.  
- **Administration**: Manage staff, permissions, and system settings.  
- **Archives & Backup/Restore**: Securely archive data and restore backups when needed.
